Install Koda on iPhone / iPad
Method 1: Apple WebClip Profile Recommended

Installs Koda directly onto your iPhone Home Screen with custom icon and borderless standalone app window.

1. Download iOS Profile
Next: Tap Allow → Open iOS Settings → Tap Profile Downloaded → Tap Install.
Method 2: Safari Add to Home Screen

If you prefer not installing a profile:

  1. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow up) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right.

Install Koda on Mac (macOS)
Method 1: Safari “Add to Dock” Recommended

Runs Koda as a native borderless macOS desktop app with Dock icon, menu bar, and window controls:

  1. In Safari on your Mac, click File in the top menu bar (or the Share button).
  2. Select Add to Dock...
  3. Click Add. Koda will appear immediately in your macOS Dock & Launchpad!
Method 2: Standalone macOS Bundle (ZIP)

Includes Koda.app, double-clickable koda-mac.command, and background LaunchAgent service scripts:

Download koda-mac.zip
How to run: Unzip and double-click koda-mac.command or Koda.app in Finder.
Method 3: 24/7 Background Service
Runs Koda continuously on boot via macOS launchd:
./install-mac-service.sh
Supports macOS Sequoia, Sonoma, Ventura, Monterey & Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) + Intel Macs.
Install Koda on Android
Direct Website Install

Installs Koda directly to your Android home screen and app drawer with custom icon, splash screen, zero browser address bar, and offline support.

If your browser doesn't prompt automatically:
  1. In Chrome, Samsung Internet, or Edge, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. Koda will appear immediately in your phone apps!
Installs as an official standalone Android WebAPK application.
